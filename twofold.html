Use arrow keys to move between cells. Press Enter to pick a tile, then press Enter on a matching neighbor to merge. Escape cancels.

Undo is free. Hints cost a star. Progress saves on this device.

How to play

  • Numbers build by doubling: 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64, 128.
  • Early boards start with only 1s. Later boards mix in bigger numbers, and a tile only merges with its exact match.
  • Tap a tile, then tap a matching tile beside it (up, down, left or right). You can also drag one onto the other.
  • The tile you move leaves an empty cell behind, and empty cells never refill.
  • Plan ahead. A tile cut off from its matches is stuck for good.
